Babel for macOS 14.2 and later

This installer contains the Babel menu-bar app and its local inference engines.
Install BlackHole 2ch and 16ch separately from Existential Audio to provide two
virtual audio paths: https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole

Open Babel from Applications and grant microphone permission. Select your
physical microphone and headphones in Babel. Follow the macOS routing guide
in Babel Settings to connect both BlackHole devices to your calling app.

Babel has an ad-hoc application signature and this installer is unsigned
unless a Developer ID build was explicitly requested. For a trusted download,
macOS may offer Open Anyway in System Settings > Privacy & Security after
your first attempt. Managed devices may restrict this option. Babel does not
change system security settings.

Configuration: ~/Library/Application Support/Babel/babel.toml
Recordings and transcripts: the folder selected in Babel Settings.
Disable login startup and quit Babel before moving the app to Trash.
Manage BlackHole separately using its own removal instructions.
